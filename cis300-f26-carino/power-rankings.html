Power Rankings

This table shows the ten strongest teams in the league right now, ranked by ESPN’s Basketball Power Index (BPI). I added each team’s record, net rating, and Simple Rating System (SRS) from Basketball-Reference so you can see whether the other numbers agree. All data are as of November 12, 2026.

What stands out

  • The Spurs are first in BPI and have a net rating of +11.2, so the record and the underlying numbers agree.
  • The Celtics have the best net rating in the league at +11.6, but the Rockets have the highest SRS at 13.25.
  • The Lakers have the best record at 11-2, yet they rank sixth in BPI.
  • The Thunder rank third in BPI with a +4.3 net rating, which is lower than several teams below them.

These rankings measure team strength, not the exact chance of winning the title. Read the Methodology page to see what each column means and where the limits are.

Reading the table

  • BPI: points above or below an average team.
  • Net Rating: points scored minus allowed per 100 possessions.
  • SRS: average margin of victory, adjusted for schedule.

Sources: ESPN BPI and Basketball-Reference.
